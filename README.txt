KANAK'S BIRTHDAY LOVE BOOK

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract this ZIP file.
2. Open the extracted Birthday-Love-Book-Media folder in VS Code.
3. Open index.html with Live Server, or double-click index.html.
4. Use the Back / Next buttons or keyboard arrow keys to turn pages.

YOUR MEDIA IS ALREADY ADDED
- 9 photos are in assets/photos/
- 2 videos are in assets/videos/

ADD YOUR SONG
The music file was not included in the uploads. Put your chosen MP3 song in:
assets/music/birthday.mp3
Then click the Play music button in the website.

FILES
- index.html: website pages and content
- style.css: burgundy and rose-gold design
- script.js: page turning, candle wish and music controls

This is a plain HTML/CSS/JavaScript website. No React, npm or build step is required.
